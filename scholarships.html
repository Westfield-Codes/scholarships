Current Scholarships

Henry David Thoreau Foundation Scholarship

Each year, the Henry David Thoreau Foundation selects several Massachusetts high school seniors interested in environmental studies to receive up to $26,000 for tuition to the college of their choice. The Scholarship's objective is to foster future environmental leaders. Henry David Thoreau Scholars major in many different subjects such as oceanography, green architecture, conservation biology, and alternative energy as well as engineering and computer science majors related to the environment. For more information please visit: thoreauscholar.org.

Deadline is February 1, 2026.

2024 Big Y Scholarship

Big Y Foods, Inc. is one of the largest locally owned supermarket chains in Southern New England. A family owned and operated corporation, Big Y has represented excellence in the food industry since 1936. Big Y is more than food, in tribute to both its employees and customers, Big Y offers an annual scholarship program. Established in 1981, the program includes a series of scholarships that honor the late Paul and Gerald D'Amour, brothers who founded Big Y in 1936. Big Y awards over 300 scholarships each year totaling more than $250,000. All BigY scholarships recognize academic merit and achievement. They are awarded to the most academically outstanding students in the Big Y market area. Please visit the page at: bigy.com.

Deadline is February 1, 2026.

The Horatio Alger Scholarship

The Horatio Alger Association Scholarship seeks to provide scholarship assistance to deserving: high school juniors and seniors: who have demonstrated integrity & determination in overcoming adversity, academic potential, the personal aspiration to make a unique contribution to society, strength of character and financial need. Although this scholarship is for High School Juniors, additional Scholarships are available for High School Seniors who will be attending specific universities listed on their website. These scholarships range from $10,000 to $52,000. Visit: horatioalger.org: for more information.

Deadline is February 1, 2026.

Mary A Killips Memorial Scholarship

The Mary A. Killips Memorial Scholarship is established to honor the memory of Mary A. Killips, a lifelong resident of Westfield whose love for her hometown, community involvement, and support of education left a lasting impact on all who knew her. This scholarship is designed to encourage and assist local students who share that same pride in their community as they pursue higher education at Westfield State University. Scholarship Amount is $5,000, applied to tuition for the first semester of a sophomore year at Westfield State University. More details and form: Killips Memorial Scholarship.

Deadline is February 15, 2026.

Walter S. Barr Scholarship

These awards for undergraduate education are made annually to graduating high school seniors. Students must be residents attending public or private high schools in Hampden County. The awards are given based on school records, college entrance examinations, general accomplishments, recommendations and financial need. The annual award is $3,000 for a maximum of four years. Applications are available in the Counseling Office or on their website at: HoraceSmithFund.org.

Deadline is March 15, 2026.

William R. Rooney Scholarship

This scholarship is named for William R. Rooney, a longtime educator and former Executive Secretary of the Horace Smith Fund, who advocated for financial assistance to students with special needs. One scholarship per academic year is offered to a graduating high school senior who is currently on and has been on an Individualized Education Plan (IEP) or a 504 Accommodation Plan for a minimum of two years. To qualify for this award, the student must be a resident of Hampden County, enrolled in an eligible high school, and anticipate attending either a two or four-year accredited college. One award each year is given based on school records, college entrance examinations, general accomplishments, recommendations and financial need. The student must submit a copy of their IEP or 504 when submitting their application. The annual award is $3,000 for a maximum of four years. Applications are available in the Counseling Office and on their website at: HoraceSmithFund.org.

Deadline is March 15, 2026.

CSF Westfield Dollars for Scholars

CSF Westfield Dollars for Scholars is an organization that assists Westfield residents with their undergraduate education funding. Recipients must be high school seniors or college students, attending or planning to attend college full time for undergraduate studies. Our application is available online at CSF Westfield.dollarsforscholars.org. Since all of our awards are NEED and MERIT based, a transcript and FAFSA SAI are required. Please visit csfwestfield.dollarsforscholars.org to apply. Applications open February 1.

Deadline is March 15.

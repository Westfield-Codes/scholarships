The Massachusetts AFL-CIO 66th Annual Scholarship Awards Program is offering over a half million dollars in scholarships to area high school seniors. All graduating seniors in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts who are seeking a college degree or job training are eligible to compete. To qualify, students must be a senior in high school, fill out the online application and take the required exam. Exams will be administered online and will be offered to registered students between Monday, February 3, 2025 and Friday, February 7, 2025. Study resources are available at: learnlaborhistory.com. Applicants must register in advance at: massaflcio.org/scholarships. GE-Reagan Foundation Scholarship

The GE-Reagan Foundation Scholarship offers college scholarships of up to $40,000 for high school seniors nationwide who showcase leadership, drive, integrity, and citizenship. The candidate must demonstrate the attributes of leadership, integrity, drive, and citizenship within their communities, schools, and workplaces. They must display a strong academic performance (minimum 3.0 grade point average/4.0 scale or equivalent). They must be a citizen of the United States of America. Application and information can be found at: reaganfoundation.org.

Deadline is January 5, 2026

2024 Big Y Scholarship

Big Y Foods, Inc. is one of the largest locally owned supermarket chains in Southern New England. A family owned and operated corporation, Big Y has represented excellence in the food industry since 1936. Big Y is more than food, in tribute to both its employees and customers, Big Y offers an annual scholarship program. Established in 1981, the program includes a series of scholarships that honor the late Paul and Gerald D'Amour, brothers who founded Big Y in 1936. Big Y awards over 300 scholarships each year totaling more than $250,000. All BigY scholarships recognize academic merit and achievement. They are awarded to the most academically outstanding students in the Big Y market area. Please visit the page at: bigy.com.

Deadline is February 1, 2026.

William R. Rooney Scholarship

This scholarship is named for William R. Rooney, a longtime educator and former Executive Secretary of the Horace Smith Fund, who advocated for financial assistance to students with special needs. One scholarship per academic year is offered to a graduating high school senior who is currently on and has been on an Individualized Education Plan (IEP) or a 504 Accommodation Plan for a minimum of two years. To qualify for this award, the student must be a resident of Hampden County, enrolled in an eligible high school, and anticipate attending either a two or four-year accredited college. One award each year is given based on school records, college entrance examinations, general accomplishments, recommendations and financial need. The student must submit a copy of their IEP or 504 when submitting their application. The annual award is $3,000 for a maximum of four years. Applications are available in the Counseling Office and on their website at: HoraceSmithFund.org.

Deadline is March 15, 2026.
